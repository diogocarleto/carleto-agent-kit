> Write an ADR when committing to a significant architectural choice — one a future reader would
> otherwise re-litigate because the reasoning is invisible in the code. Record it *when decided*, not
> after. Lands in `.agent-kit-local/planning/decisions/adr/ADR-<NNN>-<slug>.md`.

## Context

The forces in play: constraints, requirements, and what made this a decision rather than a default.
State what was true *at the time* — an ADR is a dated record, never updated to match later reality.

## Decision

What was chosen, in the active voice: *"We will …"*. One paragraph. If it needs more, the decision
is probably several decisions.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what is now expensive to reverse. Include the costs —
an ADR listing only benefits is advocacy, not a record.
